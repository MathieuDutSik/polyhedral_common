Rational and integral automorphism groups of a configuration of vectors, the
associated isomorphism and canonical form codes, and the decomposition of
the rational group into double cosets of the integral one.

The methods of the rational codes (standard, graph, subset,
plesken_souvignier, the last argument of GRP_LinPolytope_Automorphism,
GRP_LinPolytope_Canonic and GRP_LinPolytope_Isomorphism) are compared on
the same examples: same group, invariant canonical form, isomorphisms that
are linear equivalences, also for configurations not of full column rank,
and no isomorphism between configurations whose groups differ.
